Professional Overview

Jerry Lopez is an Advocate enrolled with the Madhya Pradesh Bar and has been in active legal practice since 2010, primarily before the High Court of Madhya Pradesh, Bench at Indore, and other courts and tribunals. Over the years, he has represented the State of Madhya Pradesh as Government Advocate (2020–21) and presently serves as Standing Counsel for Madhya Pradesh Madhya Kshetra Vidyut Vitran Company Limited (MPMKVVCL) and the Madhya Pradesh Pollution Control Board. His professional work has involved matters relating to constitutional matters, taxation (direct and indirect), civil and commercial disputes, labour laws, matrimonial and maintenance cases, service matters, arbitration, consumer disputes, motor accident claims, land acquisition, and criminal matters under various statutes. He has earlier been associated with an indirect taxation law firm handling matters before revenue authorities and tribunals across India. In addition to litigation, he has been involved in legal research, drafting, advisory work, and public interest activities, including volunteering with the State Legal Services Authority and engaging in social and human rights initiatives
